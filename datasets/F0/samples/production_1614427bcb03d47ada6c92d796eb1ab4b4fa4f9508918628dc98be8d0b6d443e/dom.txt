For stretch limousines, weddings, multi-vehicle convoys and peak dates such as prom season and New Year’s Eve, book several weeks ahead — those vehicles sell out first. Extras appear only if you add them - additional stops, waiting beyond the included allowance, child seats, or an itinerary change on the day. The <a href="/book">reservation system</a> shows the total before you commit.</div></div>
